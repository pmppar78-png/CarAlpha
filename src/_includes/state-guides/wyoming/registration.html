When to register

Under Wyo. Stat. § 31-2-201(a), the owner of a vehicle that will be driven on a Wyoming highway must register it at these times:

  • Every year, by the last day of the vehicle's annual registration month. Renewals can be done by mail.
  • Within 60 days after buying from a licensed Wyoming dealer that issued temporary permits, or from an out-of-state dealer, regardless of when the out-of-state permit expires
  • Within 60 days after a transfer under § 31-2-104(h), driving in the meantime with a signed bill of sale in the statutory form
  • Within 45 days after any other transfer, driving in the meantime with the title properly assigned to you
  • When a previous nonresident owner becomes a resident
  • Immediately, if a nonresident is driving the vehicle to, from or for work, a trade or a profession in Wyoming

A nonresident working in Wyoming temporarily or full time, with a noncommercial vehicle that is insured and displays another jurisdiction's valid registration and plates, can buy a temporary worker registration permit from the county treasurer instead. The permit costs $50 for each month of required registration, for up to 180 days per vehicle, and has to be prominently displayed.

The county registration fee

Under § 31-3-101(a)(i), each registration includes a county fee of 3 percent of a declining share of the factory price plus special equipment value, or $5, whichever is greater:

  • First year of service: 3% of 60%
  • Second year: 3% of 50%
  • Third year: 3% of 40%
  • Fourth year: 3% of 30%
  • Fifth year: 3% of 20%
  • Sixth year and later: 3% of 15%

Special equipment value doesn't include the value of an assistive device. Proportionally registered commercial vehicles and fleets pay an equalized highway use tax instead of the county fee.

The state registration fee

Section 31-3-101(a)(ii) adds a state fee:

  • Passenger cars: $30
  • School buses: $25
  • Motorcycles, autocycles and multipurpose vehicles: $25
  • House trailers and other noncommercial vehicles, by unladen weight: $5 up to 1,000 pounds, $30 for 1,001 to 3,500, $40 for 3,501 to 4,500, $50 for 4,501 to 5,500, $70 for 5,501 to 6,000, and $90 for 6,001 pounds or more
  • Commercial vehicles: a fee based on gross vehicle weight under § 31-18-401

Reductions and exemptions

Under § 31-3-101(b), fees for a vehicle not previously qualified to drive in Wyoming are reduced by the share of the year before its first operation, provided it wasn't driven illegally on Wyoming highways first. War veterans entitled to exemptions under §§ 39-11-105(a)(xxiv) and 39-13-105 can apply unused exemptions against the county fee. A veteran who was a prisoner of war is exempt from the subsection (a) fees for one vehicle, by filing a sworn claim with the county treasurer at registration.

Reduced commercial fees

A farmer, rancher, logger or well servicer whose commercial vehicle is used primarily in those operations and registered with the county treasurer pays 25 percent of the weight-based state fee, under § 31-3-101(b)(viii). Other owners of county-registered commercial vehicles who swear they'll stay under a mileage limit for the calendar year pay a percentage of that fee: 15 percent for 2,500 miles or less, 20 percent up to 5,000, 25 percent up to 10,000, 50 percent up to 20,000 and 75 percent up to 30,000. If an audit or mileage check shows the owner exceeded the authorized mileage, the owner owes twice the fee due for the actual miles, minus the fees already paid.

Title, plate and other fees

Section 31-3-102(a) sets these fees:

  • Certificate of title, original or duplicate: $15
  • Duplicate certificate of registration: $4
  • New plates after loss, mutilation or destruction of the originals: $8
  • New validation stickers after loss, mutilation or destruction: $6
  • Personalized plates, payable if issued: $30
  • Optional embossed plates: $50
  • New registration after a transfer between joint owners, or from an owner to a spouse, child, sibling or parent: $4
  • Antique motor vehicle plates: $10 initially and $2 to transfer
  • Annual all-electric vehicle decal: $100, and plug-in hybrid decal: $50

Under § 31-3-102(b), a VIN inspection or rebuilt salvage decal inspection costs $10, and one fee covers both when they're done at the same time. The fee goes to the general fund of whichever city, county or the state performed the inspection.
