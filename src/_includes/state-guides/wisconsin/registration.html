When a vehicle must be registered

Under Wis. Stat. § 341.04(1), no one may drive a vehicle with a prescribed registration fee on a Wisconsin highway, and no owner may allow it, unless one of these applies:

  • The vehicle is registered in Wisconsin.
  • A complete application with the required fee, and any required inspection evidence, has been delivered to WisDOT, given to a dealer to transmit, or mailed with postage prepaid.
  • The vehicle is exempt from registration.

While an application is pending for an automobile or motor truck registered at 8,000 pounds or less, the vehicle has to display a temporary operation plate. The exception is proof that you're driving it within two business days of the sale or transfer.

Under § 341.04(2m), a private person who has bought a vehicle or moved to Wisconsin can drive it after applying for registration and a certificate of title. A vehicle due for renewal can be driven once the reregistration application has been made. If a registered vehicle's construction or use changes so that it owes a higher fee, or it carries more than its registration allows, § 341.04(2) requires a reregistration application under § 341.32 before it's driven.

Annual registration fees

Section 341.25(1) sets these registration fees for common vehicle types:

  • Automobile: $85 a year
  • Motorcycle or moped with a curb weight of 1,499 pounds or less, and low-speed vehicles: $23 for two years
  • Autocycle: $45
  • Motor truck or dual purpose motor home: a fee based on maximum gross weight under the schedule in § 341.25(2)

For a truck, the maximum gross weight is the vehicle's weight when equipped to carry a load plus the maximum load you propose to carry. An automobile registered before September 1, 1947, at a fee below $18 keeps that lower fee plus $2.

Hybrid and electric surcharges

Section 341.25(1)(L) adds surcharges to the regular fee for automobiles and for motor trucks registered at a gross weight of 8,000 pounds or less:

  • Hybrid electric vehicle, meaning one able to use electricity and gasoline, diesel or an alternative fuel: $75
  • Nonhybrid electric vehicle, propelled solely by electricity: $100 plus an additional $75, for $175 in total

Under § 341.14(5), both types must carry WisDOT decals showing they're electric vehicles.

Municipal and county registration fees

Section 341.35 lets a town, village, city or county adopt an annual flat registration fee on automobiles and on motor trucks registered at 8,000 pounds or less that are customarily kept there. It's added to the state fees. If both a municipality and its county adopt one, a vehicle kept in that municipality pays both. Local governments report their fees to WisDOT, which collects them at first registration and at renewal and remits the money, less administrative costs.

Vehicles exempt from the state registration fee, and vehicles registered by the state under § 341.26 for $5, are exempt from local fees.

Emissions testing

WisDOT requires an emissions test for vehicles customarily kept in Kenosha, Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Racine, Sheboygan, Washington and Waukesha counties. Vehicles with auto or light truck plates are covered: model years 1996 through 2006 under 8,501 pounds GVWR that run on gasoline, natural gas or a hybrid system, and model years 2007 and newer up to 14,000 pounds GVWR, diesel included. WisDOT notifies owners when a test is due. A test can be required every other year before renewal, and within 45 days of a purchase and registration, a change of owners on the title, or a new resident's transfer. Wisconsin accepts out-of-state OBDII tests.

Plate fees

Starting with plates issued on October 1, 2025, WisDOT charges $6 per plate for new registration plates that aren't replacements, in addition to other registration fees, under § 341.135(2m). The department is reissuing plates on its own schedule and must finish by July 1, 2032. If a current plate is lost or destroyed, § 341.16(1)(a) requires you to apply for a replacement right away. A replacement costs $6 per plate, with different rules for some special and personalized plates.

Title fees

Section 342.14 lists these title fees:

  • First certificate of title: $207
  • Certificate of title after a transfer: $207
  • Supplemental title fee with either application: $7.50, which brings the usual total to $214.50
  • Noting and later releasing each security interest: $10
  • Assigning a security interest: $1
  • Replacement title: $20
  • Reinstating a suspended or revoked title: $25

The transfer fee and the supplemental fee are waived when a decedent's interest in a vehicle passes to a surviving domestic partner or an immediate family member. The supplemental fee doesn't apply to low-speed vehicles. Fast-service handling carries a separate fee set by rule.
