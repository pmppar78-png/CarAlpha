Passenger vehicle fees

North Dakota's annual registration fee for a passenger vehicle depends on two things: its weight and the number of years it has been registered. The schedule in N.D.C.C. § 39-04-19(2)(a) lowers the fee as a vehicle ages:

A pickup truck is charged on this same table using its gross vehicle weight rating. The registered weight has to be at least that rating, and the owner must raise it if the truck and its load weigh more. For this rule a pickup has a GVWR under 11,500 pounds, an unladen weight under 8,000 pounds and an open bed no longer than nine feet. A motor home, which the code calls a house car, is charged at the rate for a vehicle weighing 40% as much, with a 4,000-pound floor.

Other annual fees in § 39-04-19 include $15 for a motorcycle and $20 for a trailer that needs registration. Heavier trucks and buses use separate weight tables. When a vehicle first becomes subject to registration partway through a registration period, the fee is prorated monthly, with a $5 minimum.

Electric and plug-in hybrid road use fees

Section 39-04-19.2 adds a road use fee at the start of each annual registration period:

  • $120 for an electric vehicle
  • $50 for a plug-in hybrid, and
  • $20 for an electric motorcycle.

These fees are on top of the regular registration fee and go to the highway tax distribution fund.

Renewal dates

Under § 39-04-14.1, registration for a vehicle of 20,000 pounds or less expires on the last day of the anniversary month of its original registration, and you renew it each year. If you own several such vehicles, you can renew them all in the same month, and the fees are prorated. A previously registered vehicle bought while its registration was expired gets a full year's registration expiring in the anniversary month of the purchase. Motorcycle registrations all expire on March 31 under § 39-04-14.4.

A renewal receipt from the online service counts as prima facie proof of registration for 15 days. Under § 39-04-15, a registration is delinquent as soon as the old one expires. Driving with unpaid fees or without current plates or tabs violates § 39-04-37.

Title fees and excise tax

Chapter 39-05 sets a $5 fee for a title application, and $5 for a duplicate title or a title transfer. The state also charges a motor vehicle excise tax under § 57-40.3-02 of 5% of the purchase price of any vehicle bought in or outside North Dakota that must be registered here. Disabled veterans who acquire vehicles under the federal program in 38 U.S.C. 3901 are exempt from sales or use tax, and the exemption extends to up to two passenger vehicles or pickups of 26,000 pounds or less.

Selling or replacing a vehicle

When you sell a registered vehicle, § 39-04-36 lets you transfer the registration for a $5 fee and get credit for the unused months: one-twelfth of the annual fee for each full month left. The credit applies to the replacement vehicle's fees and can't run past the original expiration date. You must apply within 30 days of buying the replacement. You keep and reuse the plates you removed. The department may let you assign the credit to a spouse, sibling, parent, grandparent, child or grandchild.

New residents

Section 39-04-18 treats you as a resident for registration purposes if you're employed or work in a trade or profession in North Dakota and own, rent or live in a residence here for work, or if you stay in the state for at least 90 consecutive days. Students and certain daily commuters from reciprocal states are excluded. Once you're a resident, out-of-state plates no longer exempt your vehicle. If an officer finds you driving in violation of this rule, § 39-04-18.1 requires you to buy a full annual registration at the undiscounted rate, which the officer can issue on the spot.
