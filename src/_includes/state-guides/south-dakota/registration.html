Where you title and register

In South Dakota, titling and registration go through your county treasurer's office, and the fees are paid there, according to the Department of Revenue (DOR). The record owner, or an authorized agent with a power of attorney attached, signs the Application for Motor Vehicle Title & Registration. After buying a vehicle, you have 45 days to update the title.

Annual license fees for cars, pickups and vans

SDCL 32-5-6 bases the fee for a noncommercial automobile, pickup or van on the manufacturer's shipping weight, including accessories. DOR's fee schedule charges less once a vehicle is 10 years old:

The schedule is staggered by month, at $3, $6, $9 or $12 a month for the four weight classes when a vehicle is under 10 years old, and $2.10, $4.20, $6.30 or $8.40 a month after that. Pickups over 6,000 pounds may register by gross weight.

Other noncommercial vehicles pay by gross weight under SDCL 32-5-6.3: $120 for 8,000 pounds or less, plus $12 for each additional 2,000 pounds up to 20,000 pounds. Motor homes pay by shipping weight under SDCL 32-5-6.1, from $108 for 6,000 pounds or less to $180 for 8,001 to 10,000 pounds, plus $36 for each additional 2,000 pounds.

Electric vehicle fee

SDCL 32-5-188, as amended in 2026, adds an annual $100 fee for an electric motor vehicle, paid at registration. It covers noncommercial vehicles powered solely by an electric motor drawing on a rechargeable battery that may legally run on interstate highways. Electric motorcycles that pay the motorcycle fee are exempt, and the money goes to the state highway fund.

Motor vehicle excise tax

SDCL 32-5B-1 imposes a 4% excise tax on the purchase price of a motor vehicle bought for use on South Dakota roads and required to be registered there. It applies in place of the state's sales and use taxes on those vehicles, and failing to pay it in full is a Class 1 misdemeanor.

DOR assesses late charges from the purchase date. Interest is 1.25% or $5, whichever is greater, for the first month, and 1.25% a month after that, on applications made more than 45 days after purchase. A one-time penalty of 10% of the tax or $10, whichever is greater, applies after 60 days. For a vehicle leased for more than 28 days, the lessor titles and licenses it, and the lessor or lessee pays the excise tax (SDCL 32-5B-1.1).

Renewals

Plates renew each year in a month set by the first letter of your last name. Names starting with A or B renew in January, C through E in February, and F, G or J in March. H, I or O renew in May, K or L in June, M or N in July, P through R in August, S in September and T through Z in November.

You can renew at the county treasurer's office, by mail, online through the Vehicle Registration & Plates portal with a valid South Dakota driver's license or ID, or at a DMV Now kiosk in Sioux Falls, Rapid City, Pierre, Aberdeen, Mitchell, Watertown or Yankton. Online renewals add a $1.50 mailing fee per registration plus a processing fee of $0.95 for e-check or 2.5% by debit or credit card.

Temporary permits

A private seller must get a free temporary permit that lets the buyer drive between purchase and titling, and failing to give it to the buyer is a Class 2 misdemeanor. If the buyer needs more time, a 5- to 15-day permit costs $1 a day, with a $5 minimum. A plate ordered permit covers the wait after you apply for special plates and is valid for 30 days or until the plates arrive, whichever comes first.
