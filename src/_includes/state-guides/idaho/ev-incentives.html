No state purchase incentive

Idaho doesn't offer drivers a rebate, tax credit or other state incentive for buying or leasing an electric vehicle. The Alternative Fuels Data Center's Idaho list is mostly laws and regulations: an annual fee, rules for neighborhood electric vehicles, and limits on how charging is regulated. The state programs it does list are aimed at fleets and highway planning.

The yearly EV and plug-in hybrid fee

Under Idaho Code § 49-457, Idaho collects two flat surcharges on top of every other registration fee:

  • Electric vehicle fee: $140 a year for a vehicle powered only by electricity
  • Plug-in hybrid fee: $75 a year for a vehicle that runs on both grid electricity and conventional fuel

Neighborhood electric vehicles are exempt from the $140 fee. The money goes into the highway distribution account set up in § 40-701 and is shared out the same way as other highway revenue. The legislature added the fee in 2015 and amended it in 2017. The statute sets both amounts as fixed dollar figures, so they stay the same until lawmakers change them.

Conventional hybrids that can't plug in aren't covered by either definition, so they pay only the regular registration fee for their age.

What an EV owner pays at renewal

The passenger-vehicle registration fee in § 49-402 is based on age: $69 for a vehicle one or two years old, $57 at three to six years, and $45 at seven years or more. Adding the surcharge gives these state amounts:

  • An all-electric car in its first two years pays $209 ($69 plus $140)
  • The same car at three to six years old pays $197
  • A plug-in hybrid in its first two years pays $144

Your county adds its own registration administrative fee, and ITD lists other possible charges such as plate fees and highway district fees in some counties. Your renewal notice will show the exact total.

Neighborhood electric vehicles

Idaho defines a neighborhood electric vehicle (NEV) as a four-wheeled, electrically powered vehicle that produces no emissions and meets the federal low-speed vehicle standard in 49 CFR 571.500. An NEV has to be titled, registered and insured like any other vehicle, and only a licensed driver may operate one. It can't be driven on a highway with a speed limit above 35 mph or cross a highway posted above 45 mph. NEVs register under the same age-based schedule in § 49-402 but skip the $140 EV fee.

Charging rules

Since 2015, Idaho Code § 61-119 has kept businesses and other entities that sell electricity for EV charging outside the jurisdiction of the Idaho Public Utilities Commission. A shop or property owner that charges drivers for a charging session isn't regulated as a utility for that reason alone.

In 2025, House Bill 86 added § 39-4109B, which bars cities and counties from requiring EV charging stations, designated EV parking spaces, upgraded electrical conduit or similar infrastructure in new building plans. Any existing local ordinance with such a requirement was preempted. Builders and owners can still add chargers voluntarily, but a local code can't make them do it.

State programs for fleets and highways

The Idaho Department of Environmental Quality's vehicle replacement program offers competitive rebates for replacing medium- and heavy-duty diesel trucks, buses and similar equipment with new diesel or alternative fuel models. When a diesel vehicle is replaced with an EV, charging equipment can qualify too. The program is funded by Idaho's share of the Volkswagen Environmental Mitigation Trust and the Diesel Emissions Reduction Act. It isn't open to private cars.

ITD also prepared yearly EV infrastructure deployment plans for fiscal years 2022 through 2026 under the federal NEVI formula program.

No emissions testing to worry about

The Treasure Valley's vehicle inspection and maintenance programs, ended after the legislature repealed the state requirement effective July 1, 2023. The Department of Environmental Quality notes that EPA approved removing the program in Ada County the same year. No Idaho vehicle needs an emissions test now, whatever its fuel.
