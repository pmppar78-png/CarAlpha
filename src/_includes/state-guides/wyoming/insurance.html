Wyoming's insurance requirement

Under Wyo. Stat. § 31-4-103(a), the owner of a motor vehicle that is or must be registered can't drive it, or let anyone else drive it, without a motor vehicle liability policy in effect. A bond in the amounts set by § 31-9-102(a)(xi) also satisfies the requirement, and some vehicles must instead meet the amounts in § 31-20-107(b) and (c).

Minimum liability limits

Under § 31-9-405(b), an owner's policy has to name or reference each covered vehicle. It must cover the named insured and anyone driving a covered vehicle with express or implied permission, other than persons excluded under § 26-35-105, in the United States and Canada. The limits for each vehicle, exclusive of interest and costs, are:

  • $25,000 for bodily injury to or death of one person in any one accident
  • $50,000 for bodily injury to or death of two or more people in any one accident, subject to the per-person limit
  • $20,000 for injury to or destruction of others' property in any one accident

An operator's policy covers the named insured while driving vehicles the insured doesn't own, with the same territory and limits.

Insurance identification cards

Under § 31-8-201, the insurer issues an identification card for each insured vehicle when it issues the policy. It can instead give temporary evidence of insurance, good for up to 60 days, and send the card within 60 days. The card or temporary evidence must be carried in the vehicle at all times.

The card shows the insurer, the insured, the coverage's effective date, and the vehicle's year, trade name and VIN. Temporary evidence shows the same information except the effective date. You can carry the card electronically if you and your insurer both agree and it contains the required information.

Showing proof to an officer

Under § 31-4-103(b), a police officer issuing a citation for a moving violation, or inspecting a vehicle, must ask the driver for evidence of insurance or a bond. Showing a valid insurance identification card counts as compliance. If you can't show proof, you have seven days to produce it.

A driver or owner charged under this section can't be convicted after producing in court a liability policy, or evidence of a bond on file with the department, that was valid at the time of the arrest or citation. The judge may also order a driver who fails to produce proof to pay restitution.

Section 31-4-103(e) has the Wyoming Department of Transportation run an online insurance verification system. It's available 24 hours a day to check the insurance status of any Wyoming-registered vehicle by VIN, policy number, owner's name or other identifiers. Authorized department staff, courts, law enforcement and authorized insurers can use it.

Penalties

Driving without the required coverage is a misdemeanor under § 31-4-103(a), punishable by up to six months in jail, a fine of $500 to $1,500, or both. For a second or later violation, the judge must order the registration and license plates delivered to the county treasurer where the citation was issued. They stay there until the judge finds that you've met all your legal obligations. The judge may suspend part or all of a sentence and order probation, with a condition that the registration and plates go to the county treasurer.

Excusable neglect or a mistake by someone else is a defense, and a court may consider that evidence in setting a penalty even after a conviction. After a conviction, § 31-4-103(c) requires you to file and maintain proof of financial responsibility for three years. If the proof isn't filed within 30 days after notice, your driver's license is suspended until the department receives it.

Exceptions and nonresidents

Section 31-4-103(d) excludes self-insurers under § 31-9-414, self-insured government vehicles, vehicles meeting §§ 31-9-408 and 31-9-409, and commercial vehicles registered in Wyoming and other jurisdictions if they're insured under another jurisdiction's laws. A nonresident's vehicle registered in a state that requires insurance is covered if it meets that state's insurance laws, and one registered in a state that doesn't require insurance is exempt.

Uninsured motorist coverage

Under § 31-10-101, a liability policy for a vehicle registered or principally garaged in Wyoming must include uninsured motorist coverage for bodily injury and death, at the limits in § 31-9-102(a)(xi). The named insured may reject it. After a rejection, the same insurer doesn't have to include it at renewal unless the named insured asks for it in writing.

Under § 31-10-102, an "uninsured motor vehicle" includes an insured vehicle whose insurer can't pay within its limits because of insolvency. That protection covers accidents during the policy period when the uninsured motorist coverage is in effect.
