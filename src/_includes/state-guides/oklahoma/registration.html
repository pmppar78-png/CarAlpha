Annual registration fees

Oklahoma registration is handled by Service Oklahoma, either at its office on N Classen in Oklahoma City or at a Licensed Operator location (formerly called a tag agency). For standard vehicles, the fee depends on the number of years the vehicle has been registered. Service Oklahoma's fee page lists:

Commercial trucks of up to 15,000 pounds laden weight pay $106 a year in years 1 through 5, $58 in year 6 and $35 from year 7 on. Heavier trucks and truck-tractors pay by laden weight, from $131 for 15,001–18,000 pounds up to $1,089 for 89,001–90,000 pounds.

Motor vehicle excise tax

Oklahoma charges an excise tax under 68 O.S. § 2103 when legal ownership of a vehicle changes and when a vehicle is registered in the state for the first time. For a new vehicle, the rate is 3.25% of value. For a used vehicle, it's $20 on the first $1,500 of value plus 3.25% of the rest. The tax is rounded to the nearest dollar.

The tax is delinquent after the 30th day following the date you get ownership or possession. After that, a penalty of $1 a day is added, capped at the amount of the tax.

Service Oklahoma offers a Sales and Excise Tax Calculator. It's based on NADA pricing, so the final amount can differ, and the estimate doesn't include the other fees due at registration.

Excise tax exemptions on Service Oklahoma's fee page include:

  • Family transfers: gifts between parents and children or between spouses, with Form 794, the Family Affidavit. Siblings and grandparents don't qualify.
  • 100% disabled veterans: one vehicle every three years, with a DAV sales tax exemption card or a VA letter.
  • Trusts, divorce, and dropping a name from a title, with the documents the page lists.

The Tax Commission's rule at Okla. Admin. Code 670:20-45-5 adds the exemption for new residents. A vehicle you bring in from a former home state is exempt if you owned and registered it there at least 60 days before it had to be registered in Oklahoma. Nonresident members of the Armed Forces stationed in Oklahoma can register a vehicle already registered by them in another state without excise tax, and the 60-day limit doesn't apply.

Deadlines and late penalties

Since November 1, 2023, Service Oklahoma says you must title a vehicle within two months of buying it and register it at Service Oklahoma or a Licensed Operator. The two months follow the calendar, so a vehicle bought on November 1, 2023 had to be registered by January 1, 2024. The registration receipt must stay in the vehicle at all times.

Renewals, and out-of-state transfers in the same name, have a 30-day grace period. Penalties generally start at $1 a day and top out at $100 a year, though some classes, such as manufactured homes and commercial trucks, may be charged at a different rate. Service Oklahoma says penalties can't be waived by law.

Moving to Oklahoma

If you've moved from another state, you must register your vehicle. Service Oklahoma's out-of-state transfer page lists Form 701-6, the Application for Oklahoma Certificate of Title, among the documents to bring. If you bought the vehicle out of state, bring the purchase agreement or Form 722-1, the Declaration of Vehicle Purchase Price. The agency suggests bringing copies, since originals may not be returned.

After you pay, you'll get a standard plate and decal. If there's a lien, the title goes to the lienholder. If there isn't, the title is mailed to you within two business days. A specialty plate is ordered at the visit and can take up to 20 days to arrive.

Plates

Oklahoma started issuing the Iconic Oklahoma Plate as its standard design on September 1, 2024. Existing plates aren't automatically replaced. To switch, you pay a $4 replacement fee with your annual renewal, or $9 at another time at a Licensed Operator.

Electric and plug-in hybrid fees

Under 68 O.S. § 6511, as summarized by the Alternative Fuels Data Center, EVs and plug-in hybrids pay an annual fee on top of standard registration. For vehicles of 6,000 pounds GVWR or less, it's $110 for an EV and $82 for a plug-in hybrid. The Electric Vehicles page has the full weight table.
