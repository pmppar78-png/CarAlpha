What Louisiana offers EV owners

The Alternative Fuels Data Center's Louisiana listing includes a road usage fee, rules for charging stations and the state's federal charging plan. It shows no state rebate or income tax credit for buying a plug-in car.

The road usage fee

R.S. 32:461 charges $110 a year for each electric vehicle and $60 a year for each hybrid that is driven on Louisiana highways and must be registered in the state. The law defines a hybrid as a vehicle that uses gasoline, diesel or special fuels together with an electric motor, so conventional hybrids pay the fee as well as plug-in models. School buses that mainly carry Louisiana students are exempt. The fee was created by Act 578 of 2022, took effect January 1, 2023, and was revised by Act 155 of 2024.

Seventy percent of the money goes to the Transportation Trust Fund for road and bridge preservation projects in the Highway Priority Program. The other 30% goes to the Parish Transportation Fund for local governments.

How the fee is billed

Under OMV Policy 130.00, effective April 20, 2026, the OMV marks electric vehicles "EV" and hybrids "HV" on their records at registration. Notices go out on April 1 and May 1 and bill the fee for the previous calendar year. Each notice shows the vehicle, the date it was acquired, how many months are being charged and the amount. Payment is due June 30 of the year the notice is issued. If you sold or disposed of the vehicle before the end of the year and send that information in, the OMV recalculates the bill. In that case the fee is due by August 30, or 30 days after the adjustment, whichever is later.

The fee also applies to vehicles registered in another state that must be registered in Louisiana, such as company cars driven by resident employees. Off-road vehicles used only off-road are exempt.

The Louisiana Department of Revenue's road usage fee page tells businesses to file Form R-19000 or pay through LaTAP. For the 2025 tax year, it set a deadline of May 15, 2026. The fee can also be paid through the OMV's online form. LDR warns you to pay only once, through one channel.

Partial-year amounts

If a vehicle was registered for less than a full year, you pay only for the months it was registered, and any part of a month counts as a whole month. The published schedule:

  • Electric: $9 for 1 month, $28 for 3, $55 for 6, $82 for 9, $110 for 12
  • Hybrid: $5 a month, so $15 for 3 months, $30 for 6, $45 for 9 and $60 for 12

Charging stations

Under R.S. 45:1622 and Public Service Commission Docket R-36131, a business that provides EV charging isn't defined as a public utility and isn't subject to restrictions on reselling electricity. A 2022 law sets state policy to build out a statewide charging network. It encourages the Public Service Commission to create a rate structure for public DC fast chargers that makes prices clearer, and to deregulate charging stations. Since 2023, the Louisiana Commission of Weights and Measures has had authority to regulate the meters chargers use to measure electricity and to investigate how they're used.

Federal charging plan

The Louisiana Department of Transportation and Development filed yearly EV Infrastructure Deployment Plans under the federal NEVI Formula Program for fiscal years 2022 through 2026. DOTD's Electric Vehicle Infrastructure website has the plans and updates.

Federal credits

For vehicles acquired after September 30, 2025, the federal new, used and commercial clean vehicle credits are no longer available. Before you buy, check the IRS for current details on any federal program that might still apply.
